Listening Reader

Adding a paper or book

  1. On your Mac, run readalong.py on the PDF. It writes a cleaned .reader.json next to it:
    python3 readalong.py "paper.pdf"
  2. For the natural voice, add --audio. It also writes MP3s with the timing for the highlight:
    python3 readalong.py "paper.pdf" --audio
  3. Tap Add files here and pick the .reader.json, plus the .mp3 files if you made them. Select them together. If they're in iCloud Drive, you can add them on your phone and on your laptop.

Plain .txt files work too. Separate paragraphs with a blank line, start a heading line with #, and put a line like Page 12 to set the page number.

Files you add stay on this device; nothing is uploaded. After the first visit the reader works fully offline. On iPhone, open it in Safari, use Share › Add to Home Screen, and add your files from the new icon. To sync your place between devices, connect GitHub under Voice and text settings in any book.
